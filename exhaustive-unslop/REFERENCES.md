## Synthesis for ScreenExtend
- **Show the real app once, large.** Real screenshots replace the drawn, animated device mockups.
- **Pairing is a real sequence**, so numbered steps are legitimate here. Keep them as a plain ordered list with no animation.
- **Platform blocks:** filename-level labels, minimum OS under the button, and honest "not yet" states.
- **Specifications as a definition list** (spec-sheet voice) instead of an icon-card grid.
